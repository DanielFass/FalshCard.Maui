This is a flashcard app, kind of like Anki. You can create decks, add words to them (like a word and its translation), and then study them one by one. When you study a deck, you see one side of the card, click "Show" to see the other side, and then choose "Again" if you want to see that word again or "Done" if you got it right and want to move to the next one. When you finish all the cards, it tells you the deck is finished. You can also delete decks and words you don't need anymore.
